PROJECT OVERVIEW

⚛️ What Is QRNG Hub?

The Quantum Random Number Generator Hub is an interactive web platform that bridges quantum computing theory and hands-on understanding. By connecting to IBM Quantum hardware via Qiskit, the platform generates genuinely unpredictable numbers rooted in quantum mechanical uncertainty — something no classical computer can truly replicate.

Classical computers use deterministic algorithms like Linear Congruential Generators (LCG) that only appear random. Quantum randomness exploits the fundamental superposition and measurement collapse of qubits — making each result provably unpredictable, with deep implications for cryptography, security, and scientific simulation.
