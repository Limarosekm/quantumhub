Quantum

Each bar shows how much value[n] predicts value[n+lag]. LCG generators show spikes at regular lags due to their linear recurrence structure. Quantum randomness stays flat near zero across all lags — each number is truly independent of all previous ones.

Each point plots value[n] vs value[n+1]. True randomness fills the square uniformly. LCG generators leave diagonal lines or banding patterns — a structural weakness not present in quantum randomness.
